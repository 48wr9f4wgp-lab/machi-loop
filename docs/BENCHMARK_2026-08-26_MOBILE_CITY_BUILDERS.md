# MACHI LOOP — Mobile City-Builder Benchmark Snapshot

Verified: 2026-08-26. Must be refreshed before final RC visual/market decisions.

## Product lens
MACHI LOOP is portrait-first, touch-first, offline-first stylized 3D city management. Differentiator: **player draws strategic main roads; local roads/buildings/growth emerge automatically.**

Benchmark dimensions: mobile city-builder convention, premium/offline fit, simulation clarity, touch/game-feel, visual growth/readability, retention/progression and failure patterns.

The 2026-08-26 research used multiple active references rather than copying one title. SimCity BuildIt was used for HUD hierarchy, city-state readability, reward presentation and visual density—not its F2P/LiveOps economy. Pocket City 2-class premium city builders were used for premium/offline expectations and direct city interaction. Mini Motorways-class road games informed road legibility and immediate cause/effect.

## MACHI LOOP quality bar
- Main-road action is obvious within seconds.
- Autonomous growth visibly follows the road decision.
- Dense city remains readable on portrait mobile.
- Traffic/economy pressure is understandable and recoverable.
- Reward/tier progression creates visible city identity.
- Monetization must not undermine the premium/offline product promise.
