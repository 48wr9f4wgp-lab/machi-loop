# MACHI LOOP — Mobile City-Builder Live Benchmark

Verified: 2026-08-26
Purpose: pre-RC product / UX / visual / monetization quality bar
Method: current App Store / Google Play / official product pages. Public store evidence only; this document does **not** claim an exact global revenue ranking.

## Product lens

MACHI LOOP is a portrait-first, touch-first, offline-first stylized 3D city-management game whose key differentiation is:

> The player draws strategic main roads; local roads/buildings/growth emerge automatically.

The benchmark therefore separates:
- mobile city-builder convention;
- premium/offline fit;
- simulation clarity;
- touch/game-feel quality;
- visual growth/readability;
- failure patterns to avoid.

## Final reference set — 5 roles

### A. Pocket City 2 — **business model + mobile city-builder UX**

- portrait support;

- directly exposes road-control touch friction we can outperform.

### B. SimCity BuildIt — **mobile convention + polish + city readability**
Why selected:

### C. TheoTown — **simulation / traffic depth ceiling**
Why selected:

### D. Outlanders 2 — **stylized 3D visual/audio quality**
Why selected:
- clean vibrant visuals;
- approachable management;
- expressive animation and crisp sound are explicitly part of its product promise.

### E. Townscaper — **input-to-autonomous-transformation delight**
Why selected:
- best transferable principle for MACHI LOOP's 2+3 hybrid:
  a tiny player action should trigger an outsized, beautiful, understandable world reaction.

## Strong conventions extracted

Observed across multiple relevant titles:
1. A city action must visibly change the world immediately.
2. City growth requires a clear visual hierarchy from low-density to metropolis.
3. Mobile construction controls must be forgiving; road drawing is a known friction point.
4. Goals/tasks are useful when they point at the next meaningful city decision, not when they become chores.
5. Save/recovery quality is product quality, not backend plumbing.
6. Dense management information must be compressed for touch screens.
7. Premium/offline city builders can compete on trust by avoiding forced waits/ads.
8. Audio/motion polish materially changes perceived quality of otherwise simple actions.
9. Important state should not rely on color alone.
10. Desktop-style UI/keyboard assumptions are a mobile-port failure mode.

## MACHI LOOP changes implied by the benchmark

Already aligned:
- premium/offline-first direction;
- strategic roads rather than every local street;
- autonomous local growth;
- traffic/economy recovery loops;
- local save safety;
- stylized 3D;
- short mobile sessions.

Must be proven before RC:
- road drawing feels better than common touch-road complaints;
- each arterial decision produces a satisfying autonomous visual chain reaction;
- P1→P5 skyline/density progression is obvious;
- HUD remains legible over dense city;
- reward/audio/haptic hierarchy feels intentional;
- no color-only critical state;
- no save/lifecycle regression;
- performance does not collapse in dense city;
- no desktop interaction assumptions leak into native builds.

## Copy-risk rule

Transfer only principles, information hierarchy, interaction conventions, and abstract visual/game-feel patterns. Do not reproduce competitor logos, building designs, icons, copy, sound, animation sequences, or screen layouts closely.
