# FB-3 — Operating Economy & Recovery

Status: implementation target

## Player-facing rule
Growth creates value, but infrastructure also costs money to operate. The city should reward efficient road planning instead of infinite overbuilding.

## Revenue
The existing gross city revenue already reflects population, jobs, policy and traffic condition.

## Recurring costs
- arterial road maintenance;
- local road maintenance;
- extra widening maintenance;
- modest city-tier overhead.

## Financial pressure
The displayed operating balance is gross revenue minus recurring cost. A poorly planned/overbuilt city can run a deficit.

## Recovery
A deficit must remain recoverable. Removing unnecessary road cells lowers recurring cost and returns a small salvage value. This gives the player an immediate, meaningful action even when cash is near zero.

## Guardrails
- cash is never persisted below zero;
- no artificial waiting is required to escape a deficit;
- normal early-game growth should remain comfortably positive;
- severe overbuilding, not ordinary experimentation, is what creates sustained deficits.
