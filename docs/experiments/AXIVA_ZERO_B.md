# AXIVA ZERO B — Core Fun Experiment

Status: WORKING HYPOTHESIS / non-canonical experiment
Date: 2026-09-21
Activation: `?zero=b`
Baseline comparator: AXIVA ZERO A at `?zero=1`

## Hypothesis
AXIVA becomes materially more compelling when the same road-drawing verb produces visibly different urban forms depending on the world destination the player connects.

## B signatures
- Village connection -> residential-led low-rise spread and local-road branching.
- Station connection -> commercial-led vertical center and denser urban form.
- Interchange connection -> industrial-led warehouses/logistics form and stronger vehicle-oriented growth.
- Connecting two or more destinations begins a visible central city core, preserving the long-term product runway from settlement to metropolis without implementing the full progression system in this experiment.

## Core Fun gate
Physical iPhone play should produce a stronger reaction than ZERO A:
- not merely "slightly want another road";
- target is "I want to try the station/highway/other destination too."

## Scope
This experiment does not change GDD authority, PRODUCTION_DECISION, Vertical Slice exit, Device Validation exit, or RELEASE_APPROVAL.
Normal v0.44 remains the default. ZERO A remains available for A/B comparison.
