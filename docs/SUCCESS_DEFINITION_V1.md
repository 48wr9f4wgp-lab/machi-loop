# MACHI LOOP — Success Definition v1.0

Status: Locked for Functional Build
Date: 2026-08-24

## Repeat
The player repeatedly reads the city’s current pressure, changes the main-road network or a high-level city policy/service, then watches the autonomous city react and grow.

## Delight
The core pleasure is seeing a small road decision trigger a visible chain reaction: traffic reroutes, local streets appear, buildings rise, population/jobs change and the skyline densifies.

## Progress
Progress is expressed through city size, district unlocks, skyline density, city tiers, policy/service options, financial capacity and increasingly complex urban problems.

## Return
The next-session reason is that the player has an unfinished city with visible next milestones, newly unlocked tools and unresolved city pressures that can be improved in a short session.

## Revenue
v1.0 monetizes through a premium single purchase; the base game must feel complete without ads, energy systems or recurring monetization pressure.

## Convention
MACHI LOOP should share the category expectations of a modern mobile city builder: readable city state, satisfying visible growth, clear economy/traffic feedback, unlock progression, stable save/resume and polished city presentation.

## Differentiation
The key differentiation is the **2+3 hybrid road model**: the player controls strategic main roads while the simulation automatically generates local streets and ordinary development, concentrating input on high-value planning decisions.

## Player-facing one-sentence pitch
**「幹線道路だけ描けば、街が自分で育つ3D都市シミュレーション。」**

## First-session success
Within the first session, a new player must be able to:
1. understand that the main road is their primary tool;
2. draw a road without reading a long manual;
3. see homes/jobs appear because of that road;
4. receive a visible reward or city-growth response;
5. encounter a clear next goal;
6. leave with the city saved and a reason to reopen it.

## Product-quality success
A Release Candidate is not successful merely because all systems exist. It must also satisfy:
- no startup, progression or save blockers;
- primary actions remain comfortable on a portrait phone;
- the city remains readable at high density;
- the 3D city looks production-ready rather than procedural-placeholder quality;
- the core action has audio/haptic/visual feedback;
- the economy cannot soft-lock ordinary players without recovery options;
- measured performance meets the minimum-device target;
- analytics can identify FTUE, progression and churn points without collecting unnecessary PII.
